Questions from parents and learners

Online Python tutor: frequently asked questions

Do you teach Python to kids?+

Yes. I teach Python to kids and teenagers online, usually from around age 10, using small games, puzzles and projects so every idea is something they build and can explain.

How do online Python lessons work?+

Lessons are one-to-one over Zoom. Each student gets a private portal with their lesson notes, practice tasks, quizzes and a progress report that parents can read.

I am a complete beginner. Can I still learn Python?+

Absolutely. We start from your current level with variables, loops and functions, then move to real projects at a pace that suits you.

What can I learn after the basics?+

Data science with Pandas and NumPy, machine learning, computer vision, Django and Flask web development, and interview or university project preparation.

How do I book a Python tutor session?+

Message me by email or WhatsApp with your goals and your time zone, and we will plan a first lesson together.
